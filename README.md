Relay

Stream your Chromebook screen directly to Twitch from your browser

Using it
	1.	In the Controls panel, click Capture Screen and pick the screen,
window, or tab to broadcast. It shows up in the preview immediately.
	2.	(Optional) Add overlays: click the + above Sources, choose Text or
Image, style it, and click Add. Drag it around directly on the preview to
position it. Double-click a source in the list to edit it, or select it and
use Remove selected to delete it.
	3.	(Optional) Check Mic in the Audio Mixer to add your microphone,
mixed in with desktop audio. The meter bars show live levels; click the
speaker icon next to Desktop to mute it.
	4.	Paste your stream key from the
Twitch dashboard (Settings →
Stream) into the Controls panel. Treat it like a password.
	5.	Click Start Streaming. The dot next to “Relay” and the on-screen LIVE
badge light up once Twitch confirms the connection.
	6.	Click Stop Streaming when you’re done (or just close Chrome’s “stop
sharing” bar).
Scenes (top-left) let you keep separate overlay layouts side by side —
e.g. a “Starting Soon” scene with a bigger message, and a “Live” scene with
your regular overlay setup. Click + to add one. Switching scenes swaps
which overlay set is composited onto your stream; your screen capture itself
stays the same across scenes.
